About Assist

How it works, and where your words go.

Your privacy

🔒 Assist runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded or sent to a server. Drafts you keep are stored in this browser alone — clear them any time in Settings.

What it does

Assist turns short, rough input into a structured draft: a session note, an intake summary, a referral scaffold, a progress review, a case formulation, a client check-in, a warm admin email, or a second read for risk language. It is deterministic — the same input gives the same draft. It is not a diagnosis, a risk assessment, or a crisis service, and every draft needs your clinical judgement before it goes anywhere.
